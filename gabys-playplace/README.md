# 🧸 Gaby's PlayPlace - FNAF-Inspired Text Horror Game

You are **Robin**. Three years ago, your child went missing at **"Gaby's PlayPlace"** daycare. Driven by terrifying recurring nightmares, you enter the abandoned facility tonight to uncover the nightmare truth.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Option 1: Web Interface (Recommended)
1. Open [`index.html`](file:///C:/Users/arsha/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/gabys-playplace/index.html) in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox).
2. Features:
   - Retro CRT scanline aesthetic & dynamic flashlight cursor
   - Built-in Web Audio API sound synthesizer (whispers, robotic footsteps, stingers, drones)
   - Real-time inventory tracking & tension meter
   - Auto-save state in `localStorage`

### Option 2: Python CLI (Terminal)
Run directly in your terminal/command prompt:
```bash
python C:\Users\arsha\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\gabys-playplace\main.py
```

## 🎭 Main Characters & Key Locations
- **Robin**: The grief-stricken parent determined to find the truth about their missing child.
- **Compy AI**: A green CRT terminal AI assistant possessed by a corrupt former employee who attempts to manipulate Robin.
- **Nanny 01 (Robotic Babysitter)**: An active 8-foot tall animatronic with a chest hatch containing a bloody robotic claw.
- **Forbidden Nursery**: A horrifying chamber containing victim remains and missing child clues.
- **Security Control Room**: Room with CCTV logs revealing how the animatronics harvested children's souls for money.
- **Soul Chamber**: A subterranean cavern filled with glowing, floating blue spheres—the souls of the lost children.
- **Jack**: Robin's former best friend who masterminded the murder operation for profit and killed Robin's child.

---

## 🏆 The 6 Endings
1. **Ending 1: Vengeful Redemption (Canonical True Ending)**: Kill Jack, unlock the vault, and release the souls to the light.
2. **Ending 2: Absolute Justice (Merciful Ending)**: Spare Jack, subdue him with the Stun Taser, cuff him for the police, and destroy Compy.
3. **Ending 3: Eternal Guardian (Self-Sacrifice Ending)**: Merge your soul with the floating spheres to lead all children to peace.
4. **Ending 4: Compy's Puppet (Bad Ending)**: Get tricked by Compy's voice into entering a trap, getting digitized into the main CPU.
5. **Ending 5: Claimed by the Babysitter (Defeat Ending)**: Get caught by Nanny 01's chest hatch claw in the dark corridors.
6. **Ending 6: Corrupted Partnership (Dark Legacy Ending)**: Lower your weapon, spare Jack, and join him in the shadows to become the new dark masterminds of Gaby's PlayPlace.
